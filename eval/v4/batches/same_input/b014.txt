POST BODY: All,

So... I used to keep a running list of restaurants I'd visit for a kind of one-day route through Chinatown / Little Italy when I'd visit the city. I had been keeping it updated and commenting on it on Chowhound, but I just realized it has closed down, seeming forever, and with it goes all my notes (I know, I know...).

So I come to you, fellow Redditors, to help me update it. COVID has had m ...

COMMENT: [Here's a older comment that I made](https://www.reddit.com/r/FoodNYC/comments/uvejl1/comment/i9li61p/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=web2x&context=3) on a similar topic. It included a lot of places you already have.

**Bubble Tea:**

* [Tiger Sugar](https://g.page/tiger-sugar-chinatown?share) or [Yi Fang](https://goo.gl/maps/4CinLMBp6Q2SJQ6fA) is pretty good for their brown sugar bubble tea
* [Ten Ren](https://g.page/tiger-sugar-chinatown?share) for the OG spot. They have a tea shop in Chinatown for as long as I can remember.

**Ice cream:**

* [Taiyaki](https://g.page/taiyakinyc?share) is the instagram spot, the ice cream itself is okay.
* [Chinatown Ice cream factory](https://goo.gl/maps/MvvT3h5MUyPsDwx57) is the OG historic spot
* [Minus Celcius Ice Cream](https://goo.gl/maps/WkY68EokhUgdsyNo7) has the thai rolled up ice cream. The taste is meh to me but it's still a fun experience at least once and it's a good to share option.

**Dessert options:**

* [Eileen Cheese cake](https://g.page/eileenscheesecake?share) is pretty much the perfect eat and go spot. They sell these bite size cheesecakes and have a good variety of flavors.
* [T-Swirl Crepe](https://goo.gl/maps/9nXxedCCZ21D4tny7) for Japanese Crepes.
* [Keki Modern Cake](https://goo.gl/maps/MLcBPCUgYRH4APCv9) sells whole fluffy souffle Japanese cheesecakes. Not really eat it on the go but it's nice to bring back to the hotel. It's not a super large cake but it's really cheap, around \~$15 for the whole cake.

**Dim Sum:**

I think [Dim Sum](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XDZ_H5TFoDQ) should get a honorable mention since it's literally the definition of trying a little of everything and you probably won't have that in Ohio, but it's more of a sit down and eat. It's like Chinese breakfast Tapas, you get to order a bunch of small dishes to share so you get to try everything. Usually they stop serving dim sum after a certain time.

[Jing Fong](https://goo.gl/maps/32jFTZb6K85EfLLB8) or [Golden Unicorn](https://g.page/goldenunicornnyc?share) are good choices if you get to Chinatown earlyish and if you want the full "Cart experience". [Nom Wah](https://g.page/nomwah?share) is more touristy option. The food is still pretty good though and it's a good choice for later in the day as they serve dim sum until closing.
