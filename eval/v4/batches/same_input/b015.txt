POST TITLE: Lean Piroshki with Pancakes in Breadcrumbs and Mustard

COMMENT: Procedure for making pancakes

 Take a plate, put flour, salt and sugar, mix well. Then take another bowl, put sparkling water in it and start adding the flour spoon by spoon and mix until you use up the flour. And start to baking the pancakes

Procedure for making the filling for filling the pancakes

 Take a pan and put olive oil, turn on the stove to medium temperature, then put fresh mushrooms and start stewing for 10-15 minutes, put vegetables and black pepper in the pan. After we finish stewing, we put the fresh champignons in a bowl and then grate low-fat cheese into the bowl with the champignons. We mix these two ingredients to combine well, then add ketchup and continue mixing again

Procedure for making the mixture for gluing the piroshki

 Take a plate, put a glass of water and put a spoonful of mustard and stir to combine the mustard and water

After we have done these things, we start by filling the pancakes, we put 2-3 tablespoons of the filling in each pancake, then we roll them and seal them with the mustard and water mixture. So we repeat the procedure until we run out of pancakes. After we finish the pancakes, we take breadcrumbs and dip the pancakes in the mixture of water and mustard. First we put the pancake in the mustard and water mixture and then in the breadcrumbs and repeat the procedure. Then they are fried for 2-3 minutes on one side and 2-3 minutes on the other side. And the piroshki are ready, you can start tasting

Ingredients

Pancakes

1. Flour 500 gr.

2. Sparkling water

3. Salt 1 tsp

4.Sugar 1 tsp

Fill for pancakes

1. Fresh mushrooms 1 kg

2.Lean cheese 400 gr.

3.Ketchup 100 ml.

Fried pancakes in breadcrumbs and mustard

1. 1 glass of water
2. Mustard 1 tbsp
