COMMENT: I don’t understand why so many people recommend buying a bunch of bagels and keeping them in your room. Find a good bagel place near your hotel and buy them fresh each morning, especially if they’re baked on premises. There is nothing as wonderful as a fresh bagel straight from the oven. Keeping a small tub of cream cheese might be a good idea.

I often see posts by food “influencers” on instagram recommending “cheap eat” places in the Bronx or Queens. These are all well and good, but the problem becomes *time*, which you’re also limited by. It might be valuable for you to save $3-5 for a meal, but not if it requires an hour trip on the subway to get to and from there. So, your calculation will most often have to be: where can we eat cheaply *near here*, “here” being wherever your sight-seeing is going to take you.

Having said that though, if you’re in a heavily tourist-y area, like Times Square, taking a 5-10 min walk away from the heart of the area might save you some money. Having tried to grab a last minute quick bite before a Bway play, I’ve noticed that those no-name food places in the area are severely over- priced.

If you can find an actual supermarket, and don’t mind brown-bagging it, buying a loaf of bread, some cold cuts or pb&j and having that for lunch *might* save enough to squeeze in an inexpensive sit-down meal, including a tip. If you can manage to stay in a hotel with a kitchen in the room (and I have no idea whether such a thing even exists in NYC), that would give you more flexibility to make some inexpensive meals for any time, whether breakfast before you leave, or something inexpensive for dinner at the end of your day. Not suggesting you cook *all* your meals, but if you save enough on the meals you fix yourself, you may have a bigger budget for your meals out, so instead of those mediocre $1.50 pizza slices, you can get a decent slice from a better pizzeria.

I know you mentioned no bones or dark meat chicken, but those rotisserie chickens you can pick up in just about any supermarket these days can also be a pretty affordable shared entree.
