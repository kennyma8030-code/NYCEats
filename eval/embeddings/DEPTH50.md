Generated by `depth50.py`. The depth-20 pool leaves part of each system's top 50 unjudged; those comments count as non-relevant in RESULTS_TABLES_comments.md. Here a random 150 of each system's unjudged top-50 comments were judged blind (same guidelines, shuffled, no system labels), and the share judged >= 1 is used as that system's rate for all of its unjudged top-50 comments.

Pool relevant (judged >= 1): 7,988. Unjudged top-50 comments across these systems: 9,073, sampled rate 73%, so estimated total relevant 14,587. Micro-averaged over all 110 queries.
